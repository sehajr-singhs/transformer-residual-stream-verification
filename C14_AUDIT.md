# c14 -- independent verification audit

Generated by `audit/report_c14.py` from `results/c14_audit.json`, `results/c14_z3.json` and `results/c14_ln_sweep.json`. Methodology and caveats: `audit/README.md`.

## 0. What this audit does and does not establish

- It re-derives the prover's own box decomposition and confirms the replay reproduces `baseline.json`'s verdict at every radius.
- It attacks each discharged box with dense sampling and PGD on the exact float64 model. No violation of a claimed bound was found.
- It confirms the prover's **primitives** against z3 over exact rationals.
- It does **not** provide an independent end-to-end sound bound. Both reference engines are vacuous on this model, for a measured reason (4.7e8 amplification through two blocks). That gap is real and stated.

## 4. Corrections to the record

Claims that circulated in summaries of this work and do not survive the artifacts:

1. **The ICNN penalty is not 12x.** Measured BaB worst residuals at gamma_hi=6: layer0 3.7703e-03 vs 1.8529e-04 (**20.3x**), layer1 3.5907e-03 vs 1.3371e-04 (**26.9x**). More importantly *both* metrics certified nothing -- every `gamma_certified` is null -- so 'the ICNN adds no power over the quadratic' misleads: the quadratic did not work either.
2. **The 250x conservativeness gap is not caused by the scale-invariance of `V(e')/V(e)`.** The gap is between the *monolithic margin* certificate (rho=0.04) and PGD (rho=10). `V` does not appear in that proof path at all; the margin obligation is additive, which is exactly why BaB closes it. Scale-invariance is what blocks the *growth-ratio* obligation, a different certificate that certified nothing. The two findings are independent.
3. **Strict-decrease infeasibility is a measurement, not a theorem about transformers.** 'Strict decrease exists iff spectral radius < 1' is standard Lyapunov theory. What is new here is the measured value 1.273 on the certified subspace of *this* model -- a 2-layer, d_model=32, 26,144-parameter toy transformer on a synthetic routing task. It does not generalise to transformers as a class on this evidence.
4. **The residual recursion is pre-LN with the MLP on the post-attention stream:** `x_{l+1} = x_l + Attn(LN(x_l)) + MLP(LN(x_l + Attn(LN(x_l))))`, not `x + Attn(x) + MLP(x)`.
5. **The certificate is stated for a single prompt anchor** (`x_nom = trace[0][0]`), not over the prompt distribution.

## 5. Remaining vulnerability

`backends.independent_cross_check` should be read as *partially* closed:

- primitives: confirmed against z3 over exact rationals;
- end-to-end: **not** independently confirmed. Closing it needs a second correlation-preserving prover (CROWN/DeepPoly-style backward linear relaxation). Every decorrelating method is provably useless here.
